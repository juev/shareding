ShareDing now requires Android 10 and appears in the Direct Share row of the Share menu. With no server set up or a rejected API token, sync stops instead of retrying, and saving a link tells you why. Default tags and the unread and archive options are applied when a link is sent, so changes cover queued links too. Sync errors are easier to read, Settings reminds you to save a tested connection, and the notification permission is requested on first launch.
